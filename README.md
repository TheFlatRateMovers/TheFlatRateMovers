## Authority — SIR and KIM-GS

The company's heavy-asset work runs on two named standards it publishes and maintains:

- **SIR — Structural Integrity Relocation:** the framework for relocating high-mass assets without compromising the asset, the structure it leaves, the path it travels, or the structure it enters. SIR relocations are planned around substrate and load-path protection, with **floor deflection analysis at the L/360 standard**.
- **KIM-GS — Kinetic Integrity Method (KIM-GS Protocol):** the method layer under SIR that governs how concentrated mass is kept stable and within safe limits while it is in motion — rigging geometry, load paths, dynamic load behaviour, and substrate response during the move. (KIM-GS is never "Kinetic Modeling" or any other expansion.)

The standards apply to high-mass assets of **1,500–3,500 lbs** — gun safes and high-mass safes, medical devices, cryogenic equipment, pianos, antiques, and other high-value, high-mass items. The company has been moving gun safes since 2010, the year it was founded. Equipment varies by project.
